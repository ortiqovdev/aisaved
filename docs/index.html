Privacy Policy

AiSaved Bot (@aisavedbot on Instagram · @aisavedinstabot on Telegram)
Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. Who we are

AiSaved Bot (“the Service”, “we”) identifies the music used in a video and reports the result to you on Telegram. The Service is operated by an individual developer. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at akmalbekortiqovv@gmail.com.

2. What the Service does

There are two ways to use the Service:

  • Telegram only. You send a video, GIF, voice message or audio file directly to our Telegram bot. We analyse its audio and reply with the song name. No Instagram connection is required.
  • Instagram reels. You link your Telegram account to your Instagram account once, then send a reel to our Instagram account. We download that reel, identify the music and deliver the video and the song name to you on Telegram.

We never collect: passwords, payment details, your Instagram or Telegram contact lists, your posts, your followers, your location, or the content of any message other than the media you deliberately send to the Service.

Media files

The video or audio you send is downloaded to a temporary folder on our server only for the seconds needed to extract a short audio snippet and identify the music. The file is deleted immediately after processing. We do not archive, republish or share your media, and we do not build any library from it.

5. Music and copyright

The Service never downloads or distributes full commercial tracks. It returns the song’s name and links to the platforms where it can be heard, plus the 30-second preview those platforms themselves make publicly available.

6. How long we keep data

  • Media files: deleted immediately after processing (in every case within 6 hours).
  • Account link and request history: kept while your account is linked, so you can see your recent requests.
  • After you unlink or request deletion: removed as described below.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

You are in control of your data at all times:

  • Unlink Instagram: send /unlink to the Telegram bot. Your Instagram identifier is erased from our database straight away.
  • See what we hold: send /status to the Telegram bot.
  • Delete everything: email akmalbekortiqovv@gmail.com from the address you can be reached at, or send /unlink and then a deletion request in the chat. We erase your user record and all of your request history within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

You may also stop using the Service at any time by blocking the Telegram bot; this stops all processing.

8. Security

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database with row-level security enabled and is reachable only by the Service’s backend. All traffic to Instagram, Telegram and our own endpoint uses HTTPS, and every incoming Instagram webhook is verified with a cryptographic signature before it is processed.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions, complaints or deletion requests: akmalbekortiqovv@gmail.com
